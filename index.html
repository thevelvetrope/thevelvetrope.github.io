"A New Legibility": Design Study

Arrival

PLACEHOLDER — What was the first thing you paid attention to? Write in connected prose, first person. Be specific about the actual moment: what moved, what waited, what your cursor did before you decided anything.

Dwelling

PLACEHOLDER — What did you spend the most time engaging with? The timeline above should support whatever you claim here; point at it directly.

PLACEHOLDER — What was the most common action? Name it, count it, and say what the piece did to make that action the one you kept reaching for.

Intent

PLACEHOLDER — Your impression of the intended primary goal. This is your reading, not a fact to be looked up; say "my impression is" and commit to it.

PLACEHOLDER — How does the experience communicate that goal? Cite the specific devices: what it shows first, what it withholds, what it repeats.

Duration

PLACEHOLDER — How do you think it should be interacted with over time? Answer both halves the prompt asks for: for how long, and across how many separate visits.

PLACEHOLDER — How does it communicate that? Progress indicators, chapter counts, saved state, an ending — or the pointed absence of any of them.

Lineage

Prompts 9, 10 and 11: media forms referenced, and what they say about how to act and how to feel.

PLACEHOLDER — What other media forms does it reference? Digital or otherwise: print layout, documentary film, museum wall text, a slide deck, a game.

PLACEHOLDER — What do those references tell you about how you should act? Behaviour: read patiently, click freely, sit still, hurry.

PLACEHOLDER — What do those references tell you about how you should feel? Mood: reverent, uneasy, nostalgic, alert.

Friction

Prompts 12 and 13: the most frustrating and the most satisfying moments.

PLACEHOLDER — The most frustrating part, and what makes it frustrating. The prompt asks for the cause as well as the symptom, so give both.

PLACEHOLDER — The most satisfying part, and what makes it satisfying. This chapter carries the most of your own voice; resist summarising the piece here.
